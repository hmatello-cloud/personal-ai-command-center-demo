# Personal AI Command Center — Sanitized Demo

[Open the live demo](https://hmatello-cloud.github.io/personal-ai-command-center-demo/) · [Watch the 90-second walkthrough](walkthrough.mp4)

## Business problem

Important information and work were fragmented across email, calendar, tasks, projects, notes, AI conversations, business work, career information, and research. The Command Center was designed to reduce the cognitive work required to determine what changed, what matters, where work stopped, and what should happen next.

## My role

**AI Business Implementation Analyst & AI-Assisted Builder**

My contribution included problem definition, business and functional requirements, workflow and business-rule design, AI-assisted implementation direction, implementation review, testing, troubleshooting, acceptance criteria, decision documentation, and architecture evolution. I do not claim to have manually coded the complete application.

## Operating model

**Observe → Understand → Recommend → Approve → Act**

The demo shows a fictional change being evaluated, an explainable recommendation being presented, a human approval decision, and a visible simulated state change.

## Evidence boundary

This repository contains only a standalone fictional-data demonstration and narrated walkthrough. It proves interface, workflow, decision-control, and requirements concepts. It does **not** prove live model calls, production persistence, private integrations, or automated external actions.

Production records, credentials, databases, private integrations, account information, and complete application source are intentionally excluded.
